Swing Picker

How much do you want to invest today? You get two answers: a momentum portfolio (hold ~1 month) and swing trades (hold up to 3 weeks). Choose one for this money.

A stock you own or are thinking of buying: every signal the app has, a 3-month to 1-year outlook from what similar stocks did, and a plain verdict.

How to place swing orders
  1. Next market morning, place a limit buy at the "Don't pay above" price. If the stock opens higher, it simply won't fill. That's the plan working, not missing out.
  2. Once bought, set a GTT OCO (one-cancels-other) with the stop and target as the two triggers.
  3. If neither hits by the exit date, sell at that day's close.
  4. Picks are computed from the last completed session. Use them the next trading day only.
Not financial advice. Software that measures how rules played out historically on NSE data (5 years, today's Nifty 200 members, so results lean optimistic); history isn't a promise.
